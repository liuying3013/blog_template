Buying mobile phones directly from China can significantly improve your
margins — but only if you understand how the supply chain works and how to
protect yourself at each step. This guide walks through the process we
recommend to first-time wholesale buyers.

## Understand the Supply Chain

Before requesting quotations, it helps to know who you are actually talking
to. The mobile phone supply chain in China typically involves three kinds of
sellers.

### Manufacturers

Factories produce devices under their own brand or as OEM for others. They
offer the best unit prices but expect larger minimum order quantities and
longer lead times.

### Trading Companies

Trading companies aggregate stock from multiple factories. Prices are
slightly higher, but they accept smaller orders and mixed-model shipments,
which is often the right trade-off for a first order.

### Market Stalls

Wholesale markets move fast and carry everything, but quality control and
after-sales support vary widely. Treat them as a sourcing channel only when
you can inspect stock in person.

## Verify Before You Pay

Whatever the channel, run the same checks before transferring money.

1. Ask for a business license and cross-check the registered company name.
2. Request serial numbers from current stock and verify them.
3. Start with a small trial order before committing to volume.
4. Agree on the warranty policy in writing, including who pays return
   shipping for defective units.

## Plan Your Logistics

Air freight is fast but expensive; sea freight only makes sense above a
certain volume. Most wholesale buyers start with air shipments of 50 to 200
units and move to sea freight as volume grows. Remember that batteries are
regulated cargo — your forwarder must declare them correctly.

## Work with a Long-Term Partner

The buyers who get the best prices are not the ones who negotiate hardest on
the first order — they are the ones a supplier trusts to reorder every
month. Consistent communication, prompt payment and realistic forecasts will
do more for your unit price than any negotiation tactic.
